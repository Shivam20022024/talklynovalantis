# AI Voice Agent: Unit-Wise Cost Distribution

To ensure complete transparency, below is the unit-wise breakdown of the **₹12 per minute** charge for outbound AI voice calls. 

An AI voice call is a complex orchestration of several advanced technologies running in real-time (under 500ms latency). The ₹12/min covers all underlying infrastructure, AI processing, and telecom fees.

### 💰 Cost Breakdown (Per Minute)

| Component | Description | Estimated Allocation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Telecom Connectivity (SIP/PSTN)** | Standard carrier charges for dialing out to mobile/landline numbers, ensuring high call quality and routing. | ₹2.50 |
| **2. Real-Time Speech-to-Text (STT)** | Instant transcription of the customer's voice into text with high accuracy, even with background noise. | ₹1.50 |
| **3. AI Processing & Intelligence (LLM)** | The "brain" of the agent. Processes the transcribed text and generates a smart, context-aware conversational response using advanced LLMs (like GPT-4). | ₹3.50 |
| **4. Ultra-Realistic Text-to-Speech (TTS)** | Converts the AI's text response back into a human-like voice with natural intonation, emotion, and no robotic delays. | ₹2.50 |
| **5. Platform & Infrastructure** | Cloud servers, real-time audio streaming (WebRTC), CRM integration, dashboard analytics, and database storage. | ₹2.00 |
| **Total Cost** | **Fully managed, end-to-end AI conversational minute** | **₹12.00** |

---

### 🌟 What Value Does This Bring to the Client?
By paying this all-inclusive ₹12/minute, the client does not need to worry about:
* Negotiating rates with telecom providers.
* Managing API keys for OpenAI, ElevenLabs, or Deepgram.
* Paying for idle time or maintaining expensive cloud GPU servers.
* Hiring, training, or managing human agents (who carry fixed monthly salaries, HR overhead, and downtime).

**They only pay for active, productive minutes where the AI is successfully having conversations with their leads.**
